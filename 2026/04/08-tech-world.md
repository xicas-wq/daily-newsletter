# Tech & World News — April 8, 2026 (Wednesday)

President Trump announced a **conditional two-week ceasefire with Iran**, pulling back from the brink of escalation less than 24 hours after threatening to destroy "a whole civilization." ## Tech & AI News

### Anthropic Launches Cybersecurity AI with Apple, Amazon, and Microsoft

A major new partnership: **Anthropic** has teamed up with Apple, Amazon, and Microsoft to deploy a new AI model — **Claude Mythos Preview** — designed to defend against AI-powered cyberattacks. The model has already "identified thousands of zero-day vulnerabilities, many of them critical" across all major tech operating systems.

Key details:
- Around **40 companies** will have access to the model
- Anthropic is **not planning a public release** — this is a controlled deployment
- The company is in talks with the **US government** about defense applications
- The model detects previously unknown software flaws (zero-day vulnerabilities) before they can be exploited

This is a significant step in the AI arms race between offensive and defensive cybersecurity.

### Google Adds Mental Health Safeguards to Gemini

Google is rolling out new **mental health protections** for its Gemini AI, including a system that directs users to mental health hotlines when conversations indicate risk of self-harm. The move comes after mounting lawsuits against Character.AI, OpenAI, and Google accusing their AI platforms of contributing to harmful mental health events among teens and adults.

### Google AI Overviews: 90% Accurate, But That's Millions of Errors

New research from AI startup **Oumi** found Google's AI Overviews provide accurate answers about **90% of the time**. The caveats:
- With trillions of annual searches, 90% accuracy = tens of millions of inaccuracies per hour
- More than half of accurate responses linked to sources that **didn't actually support the information**
- The 2nd and 4th most-cited sources were Facebook and Reddit

### AI-Powered Telehealth Startup Medvi: $1.8B Revenue, 2 Employees, Many Red Flags

The AI telehealth startup **Medvi** was profiled by the New York Times for its expected $1.8B in revenue with just 2 employees — a "vibe-coded" one-person billion-dollar company. But investigations revealed:
- **Fake before-and-after patient images** on their website
- Affiliate marketing using **fake doctors and AI-generated testimonials**
- Two lawsuits over **unsolicited spam** texts and emails
- An **FDA warning letter** over misleading claims about weight loss products

A cautionary tale about AI-enabled scaling without guardrails.

### AI Boom Pushes SF Home Prices Above $2 Million

San Francisco's median home price has crossed **$2 million** for the first time, fueled by the AI industry boom. The influx of AI company employees and the concentration of AI startups in the city has supercharged an already expensive real estate market.

## World News

### Iran Ceasefire Details

The two-week ceasefire between the US and Iran includes a 10-point conditional agreement. Iran's foreign minister confirmed the Strait of Hormuz will reopen for shipping. Pakistan PM is hosting talks on Friday "to further negotiate for a conclusive agreement to settle all disputes." Markets are cautiously optimistic but the situation remains fragile.

Key market impacts:
- Brent oil plunged 14% to $93/barrel
- European stocks surged 4%, travel stocks up 7%
- Airlines may see relief: jet fuel was $4.69/gallon on Monday

### Kanye West Banned from UK, Festival Canceled

The **Wireless Festival** in London has been canceled after headliner Kanye West (Ye) was barred from entering the UK over his history of antisemitism. The UK government rescinded his travel authorization, saying it would "not benefit the public good." Ye apologized for his past remarks (including praising Hitler) earlier this year, attributing them to his bipolar disorder.

### Bipartisan Calls to Remove Trump from Office

Following Trump's threat of Iranian "genocide" before the ceasefire announcement, **bipartisan calls to remove him from office** grew. While the ceasefire has reduced immediate tensions, the rhetoric has left lasting political fallout.

### Delta Raises Checked Bag Fees

**Delta** increased checked bag fees by $10 per bag, becoming the third major airline (after United and JetBlue) to raise prices as the industry deals with a jet fuel shortage stemming from the Iran war.
