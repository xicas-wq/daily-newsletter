## Concept of the Day: "TINA" (There Is No Alternative)

"TINA" is an investing acronym that became popular after the 2008 financial crisis. It describes the idea that stocks are the only game in town because bonds and savings accounts pay such low interest rates that they're not viable alternatives for meaningful returns.

**How it works:** When interest rates are low, the "risk-free" return from bonds or savings is tiny (say 1-2%). Investors look at stocks returning 8-10% historically and conclude: "There Is No Alternative" -- I *have* to own stocks. This drives more money into equities, pushing prices higher, which creates a self-reinforcing cycle.

**The TIARA challenge:** In 2022-2024, rising interest rates created "There Is A Real Alternative" -- bonds were suddenly paying 4-5%, giving investors a safe option. Money flowed out of stocks into bonds.

**Why it matters now:** Reuters reports that TINA is back in 2026. Despite bonds still offering decent yields, the explosive stock market rally (S&P 500 above 7,100!) is pulling investors back. The logic: why settle for 4% in bonds when stocks are ripping higher? The risk, of course, is that events like the Hormuz crisis can send stocks plunging overnight, while your bonds would be safely chugging along. TINA can make investors complacent about risk -- something to watch this week.
