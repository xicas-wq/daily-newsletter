## Top Stories

### 1. Ceasefire Rally: Stocks Surge on US-Iran Truce

Markets roared back on Wednesday after the US and Iran agreed to a **two-week ceasefire**, mediated by Pakistan. All three major indexes climbed sharply — the Dow posted its best single-day gain in weeks. The rally was broad-based, with over 90% of S&P 500 stocks advancing.

**But don't get too excited.** Analysts warn this isn't an "all-clear signal." The Strait of Hormuz — the world's most critical oil chokepoint — only partially reopened. Iran accused the US of violating the deal after Israel launched devastating strikes on Lebanon (killing 254 people), while Iran's president called the attacks a "blatant violation" of the ceasefire. Trump warned on Truth Social that if Iran doesn't comply, future attacks will be "bigger, and better, and stronger."

The ceasefire is fragile. Negotiations resume Friday in Islamabad, and the next two weeks will determine whether this rally has legs or was just a dead cat bounce.

### 2. Fed Split: Rate Hikes or Cuts?

The March FOMC meeting minutes revealed a **deeply divided** Federal Reserve. Some officials flagged the risk of war-driven inflation (especially from energy price spikes) and suggested rate *hikes* might be needed. Others argued that a prolonged Iran conflict could hurt the economy enough to warrant *cuts*.

The market isn't buying the hawkish talk — CME FedWatch shows a **96% probability** of rates staying put through July. But if oil prices spike again on a ceasefire collapse, that calculus could change fast.

### 3. Meta Flexes AI Muscle — Stock Jumps 6.5%

Meta unveiled **Muse Spark**, its first major AI model from the new superintelligence lab led by Alexandr Wang (brought on in a $14 billion deal). The model outscored Google's Gemini on some benchmarks and is competitive with OpenAI and Anthropic. Shares surged 6.5% — the biggest single-day pop for Meta in months. Muse Spark will power Meta's AI app and be integrated across Instagram, WhatsApp, and Facebook.

### 4. Disney Cuts 1,000 Jobs Under New CEO

Disney is trimming up to 1,000 roles, mostly in consolidated marketing, as new CEO Josh D'Amaro begins reshaping the company. This adds to the 8,000 jobs already cut since 2022. D'Amaro is expected to push for faster collaboration across divisions. The stock barely moved on the news.

### 5. Trump Media's $1 Billion Bitcoin Loss

Trump Media and Technology Group made a massive bet on crypto last year that turned President Trump into one of the world's biggest Bitcoin investors. But Bitcoin hasn't cooperated — the company's stash is now worth **roughly $1 billion less** than what they paid for it. A costly reminder that even presidential conviction doesn't move crypto markets.

## Concept of the Day: The Strait of Hormuz

The **Strait of Hormuz** is a narrow waterway between Iran and Oman, only about 21 miles wide at its narrowest point. It's the single most important chokepoint for global oil — roughly **20% of the world's petroleum** passes through it daily (about 21 million barrels).

**Why it matters for markets:** When Iran closed (or restricted) the strait during the current conflict, oil prices spiked, which feeds directly into inflation (higher gas prices, transportation costs, heating costs — everything). That's exactly why the Fed is worried about rate hikes. The ceasefire partially reopened the strait, and two ships have already crossed. But if the truce collapses, expect oil to spike again and stocks to sell off.

**Think of it this way:** The Strait of Hormuz is like a single-lane bridge on the only highway connecting 20% of the world's oil supply to the people who need it. If someone blocks that bridge, the whole system grinds to a halt and prices go through the roof.
