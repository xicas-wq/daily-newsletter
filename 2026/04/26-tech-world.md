## BREAKING: Shooting at White House Correspondents' Dinner

A gunman identified as **Cole Tomas Allen, 31**, charged a security checkpoint at the White House Correspondents' Association dinner Saturday night and exchanged fire with Secret Service agents. **President Trump, First Lady Melania Trump, and VP JD Vance were immediately evacuated** from the stage and are unharmed.

- The suspect was taken into custody; investigators are searching for motive
- World leaders condemned the attack, calling it an assault on democracy
- Eyewitnesses described "absolute chaos" as hundreds of guests dove for cover
- Trump was about to deliver remarks when shots rang out
- CNN reported from outside a home linked to the suspect

*Sources: Reuters, ABC News, CNN, NYT, Time, The Guardian, Euronews*

## Top Tech News

### OpenAI Releases GPT-5.5
OpenAI launched GPT-5.5 as part of its push toward an "AI super app" vision. The model represents another step in the rapid AI model iteration cycle, with the AI landscape now featuring GPT-5.4/5.5, Gemini 3.1 Pro, Claude Opus 4.6, and Llama 4 competing on benchmarks.

### Google Cloud Debuts New AI Chips
Google Cloud unveiled new custom AI chips this week, intensifying the AI chip wars against NVIDIA and other players. The move signals Google's intent to reduce dependence on third-party hardware for its AI infrastructure.

### Intel Delivers Strong AI-Fueled Outlook
Intel surprised markets with a bullish AI-driven forecast, as AI spending continues to surge across the industry. The chip giant is positioning itself to compete in the AI accelerator space.

### Meta & Microsoft Announce Layoffs Amid AI "Rightsizing"
Both Meta and Microsoft announced workforce reductions as AI-driven automation capabilities allow for "rightsizing" -- replacing human roles with AI systems. This trend is accelerating across Big Tech.

### Anthropic's Mythos Model Raises Global Concerns
Project Syndicate published a warning that Anthropic's new Mythos model "will make every country more vulnerable to economic disruption," calling for US-China diplomatic coordination on AI safety.
