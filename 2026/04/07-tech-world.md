# Tech & World News — April 7, 2026 (Tuesday)

## Tech Headlines

### OpenAI Publishes Superintelligence Policy Blueprint
OpenAI released a sweeping policy document yesterday laying out how the government should prepare for AI superintelligence. Highlights include:
- **Four-day workweek**: Government incentives for companies benefiting from AI to shorten the work week
- **Worker protections**: Expanded healthcare, childcare, and retirement benefits for AI-affected workers
- **Tax shifts**: Lower taxes on workers, higher taxes on AI-benefiting companies to fund Social Security and SNAP
- **Public wealth fund**: Redistribute AI investment returns to citizens (similar to Alaska's oil fund)
- **AI safety**: Give government a bigger role in managing risks from models that could enable cyberattacks or biological weapons

The blueprint comes amid growing public concern about AI job displacement. Meanwhile, Congressional bills to tax AI and mitigate job impacts are reportedly in the works, and Senators Sanders and AOC introduced a moratorium bill on AI data center construction.

### Samsung Reports Record Quarter on AI Chip Demand
Samsung Electronics projected an **eightfold jump in Q1 operating profit** to 57.2 trillion won (~$37.9 billion), smashing expectations. AI memory chip demand drove prices sharply higher. This nearly triples Samsung's previous record. Contract DRAM prices expected to rise 50%+ this quarter.

### Apple's Foldable iPhone Hits Engineering Snags
Nikkei Asia reports that Apple's foldable iPhone is facing technical problems during the engineering test phase. Issues involve **hinge design and achieving a crease-free display**. The device was expected to launch in H2 2026 alongside two larger non-folding phones, but the schedule may slip.

### Michigan Wins NCAA Basketball Title
Michigan defeated UConn 69-63 for their first men's national basketball title since 1989, snapping a 25-year Big Ten title drought. UConn fell just short of their third title in four seasons.

### Artemis II Sets New Distance Record
The Artemis II crew set the record for **farthest humans have ever traveled from Earth** — 252,756 miles, surpassing Apollo 13's 1970 record by ~4,102 miles. Canadian astronaut Jeremy Hansen urged the next generation to break this record. The crew completed a lunar flyby including 40 minutes of radio silence on the far side. Splashdown expected Friday.

### Restaurants Banning Phones
A growing trend: at least 11 US states have restaurants or bars with phone restrictions. 63% of Gen Z and 57% of millennials say they intentionally disconnect. Even Chick-Fil-A locations are offering free ice cream for turning over your phone during meals.
