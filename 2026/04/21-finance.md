## Top Financial Headlines

### 1. Tim Cook Stepping Down as Apple CEO
Apple announced that Tim Cook will transition to Executive Chairman on September 1, with hardware engineering SVP **John Ternus** taking over as CEO. Under Cook's 15-year tenure, Apple's market value increased roughly 24x. The transition raises questions about whether Ternus can reignite Apple's AI strategy, which has lagged competitors.

### 2. Fed Chair Nominee Kevin Warsh Faces Senate Grilling
The confirmation hearing for Trump's Fed chair pick carries exceptional weight as oil prices remain elevated from Iran tensions. Warsh has pushed for rate cuts, arguing the Fed should "bet" on AI-driven productivity gains -- but hawkish pressure from the war may force him to moderate. Democrats plan to challenge Warsh's financial disclosures ($100M+ in assets) and his stance on Fed independence.

### 3. UnitedHealth Group Beats Expectations
UNH shares popped 5%+ in pre-market after Q1 results showed:
- Revenue: $111.72B (up from $109.58B YoY)
- Full-year EPS guidance raised from $17.75 to $18.25+
- Operating cost ratio climbed from 12.4% to 13.8% due to AI investments
- Improved government insurance payments and better medical cost management

### 4. Semiconductor Index Hits All-Time Highs
The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index (SOX) is up 30% in just 13 days -- the largest rally since 2002. TSMC's Q1 revenue crossed the trillion-dollar mark (local currency) for the first time. Key movers today: AMD +7.8%, Navitas Semiconductor +20.6%.

**Historical note:** The only comparable rally to a new high was March 2000, which marked the dot-com bubble peak.

### 5. Tariff Refunds Begin -- But Don't Expect Lower Prices
U.S. Customs launched a platform for companies to file tariff refund claims Monday. However, economists warn consumers won't see benefits:
- UBS: "Seems unlikely anyone will rush to lower prices"
- Tariffs still expected to raise costs ~0.1% in 2026
- Walmart could receive up to $10.2B in refunds (Citi estimate)

### 6. Psychedelic Stocks Surge on Trump Executive Order
Trump signed an order Saturday to fast-track psychedelic research for PTSD and opioid addiction treatment. AtaiBeckley shares skyrocketed 22%. The named drug is ibogaine, commonly used for depression and anxiety among veterans.

## Concept of the Day: **Confirmation Hearing**

When a president nominates someone for a key position (like Fed Chair), the Senate must confirm them through a public hearing. Senators question the nominee about their qualifications, views, and potential conflicts of interest.

**Why it matters today:** Kevin Warsh's confirmation hearing is critical because the Fed Chair controls interest rate decisions that affect everything from mortgage rates to stock markets. His stance on "Fed independence" -- whether the central bank should resist political pressure on rate decisions -- is the hottest issue. Historically, markets perform better when the Fed is seen as independent from political influence.

**Connection to current news:** Senator Thom Tillis has vowed to block Warsh's confirmation until the DOJ drops its investigation into current Fed Chair Jerome Powell -- creating a political standoff that could leave the Fed in limbo during a geopolitically volatile period.
