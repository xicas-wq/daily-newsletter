## Concept of the Day: V-Shaped Recovery

A **V-shaped recovery** is a pattern on a price chart where a sharp decline is immediately followed by an equally sharp rebound, forming the shape of the letter "V."

**Why it matters right now:** The S&P 500 just did this in record time -- dropping ~9% from late January, then recovering everything in just weeks. This is significant because:

- **Speed matters**: The faster the recovery, the more it suggests the selloff was driven by *sentiment* (fear, panic) rather than *fundamentals* (actual economic deterioration)
- **Trump's influence**: Unlike typical V-shaped recoveries triggered by policy changes or economic data, this one is being driven by presidential social media posts -- a truly unusual market dynamic
- **Historical context**: The last V-shaped recovery this fast was in March 2020, when the Fed intervened with massive stimulus. This time, there's no equivalent policy trigger -- it's pure market psychology

**Think of it like this**: Imagine a bouncy ball dropped from a table. A V-shape means it hits the floor and bounces right back up to where it started. If it bounced slowly or only partway back, that would be a "U-shaped" or "L-shaped" recovery instead. The shape tells you a lot about what's driving the market.
