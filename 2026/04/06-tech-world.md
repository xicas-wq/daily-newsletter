## 🚀 HEADLINE: Artemis II Lunar Flyby — TODAY

**Four astronauts are flying around the Moon today.** NASA's Artemis II mission — the first crewed lunar flight since Apollo 17 in 1972 — is performing its **lunar flyby** on April 6, 2026. The crew will pass behind the far side of the Moon before heading back to Earth.

The New York Times, Florida Today, and every major outlet are covering it live. This is the precursor to Artemis III, which will land astronauts on the lunar surface.

**Why it matters for tech:** Artemis represents a new era of space exploration powered by modern computing, AI navigation systems, and next-generation spacecraft. The SLS rocket and Orion capsule are the most advanced human spaceflight systems ever built.

## 🔭 Science Corner: Artemis II Mission Details

- **Crew:** 4 astronauts (first crewed lunar mission in 54 years)
- **Mission:** Fly around the Moon and return to Earth
- **Duration:** ~10 days total
- **Purpose:** Test Orion spacecraft and life support systems before Artemis III lands on the Moon
- **Historic moment:** Includes the first woman and first person of color assigned to a lunar mission
- **Watch live:** NASA TV and all major streaming platforms
