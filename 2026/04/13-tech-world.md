## World News

### US Orders Naval Blockade of Iran — Escalation Fears Rise

After US-Iran peace talks collapsed in Pakistan this weekend, President Trump announced a full naval blockade of all Iranian ports beginning today at 10 AM ET. Iran has warned it will treat the blockade as a ceasefire violation, threatening to strike ports across the Middle East in retaliation. Oil surged above $100/barrel. Analysts warn this could worsen the global energy crisis significantly.

### Hungary's Orban Ousted After 16 Years in Power

In one of Europe's most consequential elections in decades, Viktor Orban conceded defeat to opposition leader Peter Magyar in a landslide. Magyar's Tisza party will control more than two-thirds of parliament — enough for constitutional changes. Orban, a key ally of Trump and Putin, called the loss "painful." Magyar, described as center-right, has pledged major anti-corruption reforms. He is set to outline his plans today.

### IMF/World Bank Spring Meetings This Week

Finance ministers gather in Washington starting Monday. The IMF's World Economic Outlook (Tuesday) is expected to downgrade global growth forecasts due to the Iran war's impact on energy prices, trade, and food costs.

### Israel-Lebanon Diplomats Meet Tuesday

Lebanon, battered by Israeli strikes, is desperate for a ceasefire. But Israel won't agree without assurances Hizbullah will be disarmed — something both Hizbullah and Iran have threatened a coup over if attempted.

## Tech & AI News

### AI Chip Shortage Intensifies

The Wall Street Journal reports that demand for agentic AI has climbed so high it's causing chip shortages, rationing of compute, and product rollout delays. OpenAI CFO Sarah Friar said she "spends a lot of time trying to find any last-minute compute available." This is bullish for TSMC and ASML, both reporting earnings this week.

### PwC Study: 20% of Companies Capturing 75% of AI Gains

A new PwC study finds that three-quarters of AI's economic gains are being captured by just 20% of companies — those focused on growth rather than just productivity improvements.

### Small Models Found Same Vulnerabilities as Mythos

In a notable finding, researchers demonstrated that smaller AI models discovered the same software vulnerabilities that Anthropic's powerful Mythos model identified — raising questions about whether frontier AI capabilities actually provide unique security risks, or whether the threat landscape is broader.
