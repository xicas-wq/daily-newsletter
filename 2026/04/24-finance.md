**The pattern:** Big Tech is simultaneously spending billions on AI while cutting human workers — the "AI efficiency" thesis playing out in real time.

### Concept of the Day: K-Shaped Recovery

A **K-shaped recovery** describes an economic rebound where different groups diverge sharply — some shoot up while others continue declining, forming a "K" shape.

**Current example:** Bank of America just found this playing out in clothing purchases. Luxury fashion and discount/thrift stores are both growing, while traditional mid-market department stores struggle. Secondhand fashion transactions grew 9x faster than spending itself in March.

Even wealthy consumers are hunting for value — Quince, a direct-to-consumer brand offering "luxury for less," just hit a $10.1 billion valuation. Meanwhile, LVMH stock is down 26% this year as CEO Bernard Arnault publicly warned the Iran conflict could cause a "world catastrophe."

**The lesson:** In a K-shaped environment, being in the middle is the worst place. Companies need to pick a lane — premium or value — and own it. The same applies to investing: look for companies serving the top or bottom of the K, not the squeezed middle.
