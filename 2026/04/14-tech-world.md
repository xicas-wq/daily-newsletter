## Tech & AI

### Meta Building AI Clone of Mark Zuckerberg

Meta is developing a photorealistic AI version of Mark Zuckerberg to interact with employees, according to the Financial Times. The AI Zuck is being trained on his mannerisms, tone, and public statements. If successful, the same technology could let creators and influencers build AI doppelgangers for social media engagement. Meta has already created AI characters modeled on celebrities like Naomi Osaka and Kendall Jenner. Separately, Meta also has a "CEO agent" project aimed at creating an AI assistant to funnel information to the real Zuckerberg more efficiently.

### Novo Nordisk Partners with OpenAI for AI-Driven Drug Discovery

The pharma giant announced a deal with OpenAI to push AI transformation in healthcare, aiming to analyze datasets at unprecedented scale and accelerate drug R&D. This follows a growing trend of pharmaceutical companies turning to AI to identify drug candidates faster and cut development costs.

### An AI System Passed Peer Review - The Scientific Community Isn't Ready

Forbes reports on an AI system that successfully passed the peer review process for a scientific publication, raising fundamental questions about authorship, accountability, and the integrity of the scientific process. The development has sparked debate about how academic institutions should adapt their standards.

### Sam Altman Molotov Cocktail Suspect Charged

The 20-year-old suspect accused of throwing a Molotov cocktail at OpenAI CEO Sam Altman's home has been charged with two counts of attempted murder and attempted arson. Court documents reveal the suspect was opposed to AI and had a list of other tech CEO targets. The FBI has raided his home.

### WordPress Plugin Supply Chain Attack

A major supply chain attack hit the WordPress ecosystem: someone bought 30 WordPress plugins and planted backdoors in all of them. This is a growing pattern where attackers acquire legitimate, trusted plugins and then inject malicious code that reaches thousands of websites. *(993 points on Hacker News)*

### Google Cracks Down on "Back Button Hijacking"

Google announced a new spam policy targeting websites that hijack the browser's back button, a long-standing user annoyance where clicking "back" either does nothing or redirects to unwanted pages. Sites employing this tactic will now face search ranking penalties. *(433 points on HN)*

### GitHub Launches Stacked PRs

GitHub introduced built-in support for stacked pull requests, a workflow where developers break large changes into a chain of smaller, reviewable PRs that build on each other. This has been a highly requested feature, previously only possible through third-party tools. *(744 points on HN)*

### Backblaze Backup Reliability Concerns

A detailed post alleges that Backblaze, the popular cloud backup service, has silently stopped backing up user data in certain scenarios, raising alarm among users who depend on it for their backup strategy. *(253 points on HN)*

## World News

### US-Iran Tensions: Hormuz Blockade Day One

The US began its blockade of ships from Iranian ports in the Strait of Hormuz, a critical global oil chokepoint. Key developments:
- Trump threatened Iranian ships would be "immediately ELIMINATED"
- Iran warned that if its ports are threatened, no port in the Persian Gulf will be safe
- China called the blockade "dangerous and irresponsible"; Xi Jinping said the international order is "crumbling"
- The IEA projected the sharpest drop in global oil demand since COVID-19
- Trump claimed Iran reached out for a peace deal; VP Vance said a deal is possible but "it's up to the Iranians"

### Trump-Pope Leo XIV Feud

President Trump faced backlash after beefing with Pope Leo XIV and briefly posting an AI-generated image on Truth Social that appeared to depict him as Jesus. The pope, who spoke out against the war in Iran, responded that he was "not afraid of the Trump administration." Trump deleted the image after pushback from some on the Christian right, saying he thought it portrayed him "as a doctor."

### Two Congress Members Resign Amid Scandals

Rep. Eric Swalwell (D-CA) and Rep. Tony Gonzales (R-TX) announced they would resign amid sexual misconduct allegations. Both were facing calls for expulsion and both have denied the accusations.

### Canadian Travel Boycott Deepens

Canadian visits to the US are down 35% since Trump returned to office, with no signs of letting up. Road trips from Canada dropped 35% compared to March 2024, while Canadian overseas travel increased 5%, suggesting Canadians are choosing other international destinations over the US.

### Clinton & Sandberg Team Up to End Child Marriage

Former Secretary of State Hillary Clinton and Meta's former COO Sheryl Sandberg are joining forces to combat child marriage, which costs an estimated $175 billion per year globally according to new analysis.
