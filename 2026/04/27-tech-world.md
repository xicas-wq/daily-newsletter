## BREAKING: China Blocks Meta's $2B Acquisition of Manus AI

Beijing has ordered Meta to **unwind its $2 billion acquisition of Manus**, the Singapore-based agentic AI startup founded by Chinese engineers in Wuhan. China's regulators cited national security concerns after a months-long probe, and last month barred the founders from leaving the country.

- Meta said the transaction "complied fully with applicable law" and anticipates "an appropriate resolution"
- The move deals a major setback to Zuckerberg's push into AI agents
- Manus had emerged as a leading agentic AI platform before the acquisition was announced in late December
- Analysts see it as Beijing weaponizing tech deal reviews amid escalating US-China tensions

*Sources: Reuters, CNBC, CNN, BBC, NYT, Forbes, TechCrunch, NPR*

## WHCD Shooting: Suspect Charged With Attempting to Assassinate Trump

**Cole Tomas Allen, 31**, a tutor and computer engineer from Torrance, California, has been charged with **two counts of federal gun crimes and one count of attempting to assassinate President Trump** following Saturday night's shooting at the White House Correspondents' Dinner.

- Allen rushed a security checkpoint armed with a shotgun, handgun, and knives, exchanging gunfire with Secret Service agents
- He sent a message to family describing himself as a "Friendly Federal Assassin" shortly before the attack
- Writings obtained by investigators reference targeting the Trump administration
- President Trump, First Lady Melania Trump, and VP Vance were evacuated and are unharmed

## Top Tech News

### Musk v. Altman Trial Opens in Oakland
The blockbuster **Musk v. OpenAI trial** began today at the Ronald V. Dellums U.S. Courthouse in Oakland, with jury selection followed by opening arguments. Musk is seeking **$150 billion in damages** from OpenAI and Microsoft, alleging Sam Altman double-crossed him by straying from the company's founding nonprofit mission. Musk dropped his fraud claims ahead of trial but the remaining charges could still reshape AI's future. The trial may run several weeks.

*Sources: NYT, ABC7, CNBC, Yahoo Finance, Fortune*

### Google Commits Up to $40B Investment in Anthropic
Alphabet confirmed a massive investment of **up to $40 billion in Anthropic**, starting with a $10 billion cash infusion that values the Claude-maker at **$350 billion**. The deal includes a commitment to provide at least 5 GW of computing power. Anthropic's annualized revenue reportedly exceeds $30 billion, with a potential IPO as soon as October 2026.

*Sources: Bloomberg, Reuters, WSJ, TechCrunch, CNBC, NYT*

### Apple Names John Ternus as New CEO
Apple announced that **John Ternus**, SVP of Hardware Engineering, will become CEO on **September 1, 2026**, replacing Tim Cook after 15 years. Cook will transition to Executive Chairman. The succession is being called one of the most carefully choreographed in corporate history, coming just as Apple prepares to enter the foldable iPhone era.

*Sources: Apple Newsroom, CNBC, BBC, Fortune, NYT, CNN*

### Intel Surges 24% -- Best Day Since 1987
Intel shares soared **24.4% to over $83** on Friday after Q1 earnings blew past expectations: revenue of $13.6B (up 7% YoY, $1.4B above guidance), non-GAAP EPS of $0.29, and gross margin at 41%. The stock surpassed its dot-com era high from August 2000. HSBC raised its price target to $100, citing agentic AI-driven CPU demand.

*Sources: CNBC, Forbes, Yahoo Finance, Motley Fool, Barron's*

### Tesla Veteran Launches World's First Autonomous Mine
**Turner Caldwell**, ex-Tesla engineer, officially launched the world's first autonomous mining operation today at **Mariana Minerals' Copper One mine** in southeast Utah. The operation uses AI-powered robots from Pronto (autonomous haulage trucks), Boston Dynamics (Spot), and Sandvik (autonomous drills) to extract and refine copper with minimal human presence.

*Sources: Forbes, TechCrunch, PR Newswire, Morningstar*

### Microsoft and OpenAI End Exclusive Revenue-Sharing Deal
Microsoft and OpenAI have terminated their exclusive partnership and revenue-sharing agreement, a significant restructuring of one of tech's most consequential relationships. The move comes as the Musk trial spotlights the growing tensions around OpenAI's commercialization.

*Source: Bloomberg (via Hacker News)*

### UK Millionaire Exodus Accelerates
The UK is losing wealthy residents faster than any other country. An estimated **16,500 millionaires left in 2025**, the highest exodus globally, driven by tax policy changes including the end of non-dom status. The Henley Private Wealth Migration Report warns the trend could "potentially double" in 2026. Top destinations: UAE, USA, Italy, and Switzerland.
