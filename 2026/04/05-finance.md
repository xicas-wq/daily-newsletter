## Concept of the Day: Strategic Petroleum Reserves (SPR)

**What is it?** The Strategic Petroleum Reserve is a government-maintained emergency stockpile of crude oil. The US SPR, stored in underground salt caverns along the Gulf Coast, is the world's largest at ~400 million barrels (down from a peak of 727 million).

**Why does it exist?** Created after the 1973 Arab oil embargo, the SPR is meant to cushion the economy during supply disruptions — exactly like the Iran war situation now.

**What's happening now?** The IEA (International Energy Agency) coordinated a record release of 400 million barrels from member nations' reserves. But here's the catch: with oil above $110 and the Strait of Hormuz effectively disrupted, these releases are barely keeping up with lost supply. It's like trying to drain a bathtub with a teaspoon while the faucet is running.

**The bigger picture:** Every barrel released now is one less barrel available for future emergencies. The US SPR is already at historically low levels after massive releases in 2022. If the Iran conflict escalates further, the safety net is thinner than it's been in decades.
