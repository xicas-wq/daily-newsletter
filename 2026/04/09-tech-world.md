## Tech Headlines

### 1. Meta Launches Muse Spark — First Model from Superintelligence Lab

The biggest AI news of the day: **Meta debuted Muse Spark**, its first flagship AI model developed under the new superintelligence division led by Alexandr Wang. This is Meta's first major model release since Llama 4 (April 2025), which was widely seen as disappointing.

Key details:
- **Codenamed "Avocado"** during development, Muse Spark is a "ground-up overhaul" of Meta's AI efforts
- Outscores **Google's Gemini** on some internal benchmarks and is competitive with OpenAI and Anthropic models
- Will power **Meta's standalone AI app** and be integrated into Instagram, WhatsApp, and Facebook
- First fruit of the **$14 billion spending spree** that brought in Wang as Chief AI Officer
- Meta stock jumped **6.5%** on the announcement

Coverage from CNBC, NYT, TechCrunch, WSJ, Reuters, CNN, Axios, and Euronews all highlighted this as Meta's most significant AI milestone in over a year. The AI arms race just got another serious contender.

## World News

### US-Iran Ceasefire: Fragile and Contested

The two-week ceasefire between the US and Iran — the first pause in over a month of conflict — is **already under strain**:

- **Iran accused the US** of violating the deal after Israel launched one of its deadliest strikes on Lebanon (254 killed), though the US says Lebanon wasn't part of the ceasefire terms
- **Iran's President Pezeshkian** called the Lebanon strikes a "blatant violation" that renders negotiations "meaningless"
- **Iran's Parliament Speaker** insists "Lebanon and Hezbollah are an inseparable part of the ceasefire"
- **The Strait of Hormuz** partially reopened — Iran says it will stay open "if attacks against Iran are halted"
- **Britain** is calling for toll-free passage through Hormuz and wants Lebanon included in the deal
- **Defense Secretary Hegseth** claims Iran's defense-industrial base has been "completely destroyed"
- **Trump** warned on Truth Social: if Iran doesn't comply, future attacks will be "bigger, and better, and stronger"
- **Negotiations resume Friday** in Islamabad, Pakistan
- Iran submitted a **10-point proposal** — key question is how it compares to US demands

### Trump Considers Pulling Troops from NATO Allies

President Trump is reportedly considering moving US troops **away from NATO members** that haven't supported the Iran war, relocating them to more supportive countries. Press Secretary Leavitt confirmed Trump has even considered withdrawing from NATO entirely (though a 2023 law prevents unilateral withdrawal). This adds another layer of geopolitical uncertainty.
