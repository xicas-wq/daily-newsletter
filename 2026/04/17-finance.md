## Concept of the Day: Earnings Beats vs. Forward Guidance

**What happened**: Netflix beat Q1 estimates by a massive margin, yet the stock fell. Why?

**The concept**: In markets, **it's not about what just happened -- it's about what's expected next**. When a company reports earnings, two things matter:

1. **The backward look** (actual results vs. estimates) -- Netflix crushed this
2. **The forward look** (guidance for next quarter/year) -- Netflix disappointed here

This is called the **"beat and lower"** pattern. Even when a company beats current estimates, if it signals slower growth ahead, the stock often sells off. Investors are always pricing in the future, not the past.

**Key takeaway**: When you see a stock drop despite "great earnings," check the guidance. The market is a forward-looking machine -- it cares more about where a company is going than where it's been. This is why Netflix can report 16% revenue growth and 62% earnings surprise, yet still see its stock fall.
