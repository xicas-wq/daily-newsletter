## AI & Tech News

### Google to Build Custom AI Chips with Marvell
The Information reports that Alphabet's Google is in talks with **Marvell Technology** to develop two new AI-focused chips: a TPU memory accelerator and a next-gen TPU. This deepens Google's push to reduce reliance on Nvidia GPUs for AI workloads. Google Cloud is increasingly powered by TPUs, and custom silicon gives Google cost and performance advantages in the AI infrastructure race. Marvell stock hit a 52-week high on the news.

### AI-Generated Song Tops iTunes Charts
An AI-generated song hit **#1 on iTunes** in the US after going viral on TikTok. The song (quiz: is it "Livin' on Borrowed Time," "Celebrate Me," "Dust and Silence," or "Praise Me"?) highlights the rapidly blurring line between human and AI-created content. The music industry is scrambling to establish attribution rules and royalty frameworks for AI-generated content.

### Formation Bio: AI for Drug Clinical Trials ($615M raised)
Sam Altman-backed startup **Formation Bio** ($18B valuation) is using AI to rescue stalled drug candidates and get them through clinical trials faster. CEO Benjamine Liu argues clinical trials, not drug discovery, are the biggest bottleneck. The company acquires early-stage drug candidates that stalled and applies AI to redesign trial protocols. Sequoia's Michael Moritz calls it potentially "one of these enormously significant companies."

### Elon Musk Pushes Universal Basic Income
Musk touted **universal basic income** (or "universal high income") as the remedy for AI-driven unemployment. He suggested AI and robotics will increase the money supply enough to fund it. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has also long supported UBI. Meanwhile, the "Father of AGI" warns workers to build 3 specific skills before jobs vanish (Forbes).

### Tesla Terafab: Musk's $25B AI Chip Venture
Ahead of Wednesday's earnings, Bloomberg reports Musk is pushing suppliers to move at "light speed" on **Terafab** -- a joint Tesla/SpaceX venture to build AI chips. The project aims for a terawatt of annual computing capacity and could cost $25B+. Intel (up 85% YTD) has signed on, contributing to its massive rally.

## World News

### Artemis II Crew Returns to Earth
NASA's **Artemis II** Orion capsule successfully landed off the coast of San Diego last week. At a cost of ~$90 billion, the mission achieved rare bipartisan support: 97% of Americans supported it, and 65% said following it "felt different from their usual online experience." Meanwhile, the White House proposed cutting NASA's budget by $5.6 billion (23%).

### OnlyFans in Talks for $3B Minority Stake Sale
OnlyFans is reportedly in discussions to sell a **minority stake** to San Francisco-based Architect Capital at a $3B+ valuation. The deal comes weeks after founder Leonid Radvinsky, the Ukrainian-born entrepreneur who transformed OnlyFans into an adult entertainment empire, died of cancer.

### US Navy Supercarrier Breaks Deployment Record
A US Navy supercarrier has set the record for the **longest modern deployment**, driven by the ongoing operations in the Strait of Hormuz. The extended deployment highlights the strain the Iran conflict is placing on US military resources.

### Kevin Warsh Fed Hearing Tuesday
**Kevin Warsh**, Trump's nominee for the next Federal Reserve Chair, faces his Senate confirmation hearing Tuesday. Markets will be watching for signals on monetary policy direction, especially as oil prices threaten a new inflationary wave.
