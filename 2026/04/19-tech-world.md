## Tech & AI

### Humanoid Robot Beats Human Half-Marathon Record in Beijing
A humanoid robot sprinted to victory in a Beijing race, beating the human half-marathon world record. The achievement marks a milestone in robotics locomotion, showing that bipedal robots are now capable of sustained high-speed movement over long distances. The implications for logistics, military applications, and search-and-rescue operations are significant.

### AI Wildfire Prediction Scout Launches in British Columbia
A B.C. company has created an AI "scout" system designed to predict wildfire risk. Combined with British Columbia's new lithium refining plant (first-in-North-America technology), the province is positioning itself at the intersection of clean energy and AI-driven environmental monitoring.

### Vibe Coding: One Year Later
Medium's newsletter this week reflected on the state of "vibe coding" one year after Andrej Karpathy coined the term. The verdict: AI can generate code faster than ever, but security incidents, context collapse, and a decline in craft quality have pushed the industry toward "agentic engineering" -- where AI assists but humans own and review the code. Developer Steve Yegge describes himself as a "vibe maintainer," reviewing AI-generated pull requests rather than writing code from scratch.
