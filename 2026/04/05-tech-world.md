## Tech Headlines

### Artemis II: Lunar Flyby Tomorrow
NASA's Artemis II crew is now **closer to the Moon than to Earth**, and the historic lunar flyby is set for **Monday, April 6**. The four astronauts aboard Orion will swing around the far side of the Moon and even witness a **solar eclipse** during the maneuver. This is NASA's last major moon mission without Silicon Valley — future Artemis missions will heavily involve SpaceX's Starship. TechCrunch notes this marks a "pivot point" for how space exploration is conducted.

### SpaceX's Growing Empire
Reuters published a deep dive into SpaceX's business and finances ahead of its expected IPO. The company now spans:
- **Rockets:** Falcon 9 dominates global launch market
- **Starlink:** Satellite internet with millions of subscribers
- **AI:** A budding AI division leveraging its satellite data
SpaceX's IPO filing (first reported last week) could value the company north of **$350 billion**, making it one of the largest tech IPOs ever.

### OpenAI Acquires TBPN Podcast
OpenAI completed its acquisition of **TBPN** ("the talk show every VC, founder, and CTO in Silicon Valley watches") for an undisclosed sum. TBPN will retain editorial independence while OpenAI scales it as a platform for "live conversations, product dialogue, and accelerating new ideas." Substack commentators call it a sign that even the most powerful AI companies are "desperate for storytellers."

### Chamath Palihapitiya on AI Investing
Billionaire investor Chamath Palihapitiya shared his latest views on AI investment opportunities, noting the sector is bifurcating between "foundation model companies burning cash" and "AI application companies printing money."

### Fake Virality Exposed
Musician Eliza McLamb exposed **Chaotic Good Projects**, a digital marketing agency that manufactures hundreds of fake fan accounts for musicians. Clients reportedly include major names like Mk.gee, Laufey, and Wet Leg. The agency has since scrubbed incriminating content from its website. The revelation sparked debate about algorithm-controlled marketplaces driving artists toward artificial engagement.

### Big Tech Shouldn't Write AI Rules
UNESCO's Gabriela Ramos argued in Project Syndicate that AI governance must not be left to profit-driven firms. She pointed to **India and the EU** as models for ensuring AI tools serve the public interest rather than corporate bottom lines.
